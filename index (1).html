A planning method

24 hours is too short. 7 days is too long.

Plan in 3–4 day windows. Treat your schedule like a kanban board and your events like sticky notes: lock the non-negotiables, kill what you're not doing, then place what's left by how much energy it costs you.

The board

Fill the first two columns and roughly 70% of the window is already spoken for. Only then do you rank what you actually want to do.
